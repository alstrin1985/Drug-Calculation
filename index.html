    Clinical Safety, Monitoring & Antidote Guidelines

    Critical institutional protocols for safe infusion administration

    Routine Monitoring

    • Re-check all vital signs and pump rate settings independently.
    • Monitor lab values and patient response continuously.
    • Observe closely for any adverse clinical signs.

    Adverse Reaction Monitoring

    Report any unexpected hemodynamic instability, bleeding, or laboratory anomalies immediately to the attending physician or medical team.

    Emergency Protocol & Reversal

    Emergency reversal agents and support equipment must be immediately accessible in the unit whenever vasoactive or high-alert infusions are administered.
